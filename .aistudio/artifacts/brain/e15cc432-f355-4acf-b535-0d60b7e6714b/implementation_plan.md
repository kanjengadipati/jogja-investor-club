# Punya Tanah di Jogja — Wujudkan Properti Bernilai

An interactive property development landing page and land potential appraisal application for Yogyakarta (Jogja) landowners, transforming vacant or underutilized land into high-yielding houses, villas, or exclusive boarding houses (kos/homestay) with instant financial simulation and direct WhatsApp lead dispatch.

---

### User Review & Critical Decisions

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The user confirmed the following architectural and visual foundations in Phase 1:
> - **Experience Type**: Full-funnel landing page with interactive Land ROI Calculator.
> - **Lead & Appraisal Behavior**: Instant multi-concept ROI estimate with pre-filled WhatsApp direct consultation dispatch.
> - **Aesthetic Identity**: Elegant dark emerald (`#0B251F`), champagne gold (`#C5A25D`), and clean ivory canvas, echoing the premium architectural flyer.

---

### 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: 
  A high-converting web platform specifically tailored for landowners, heirs, and property investors in Yogyakarta. Visitors explore the 3 primary building archetypes (**Rumah Keluarga**, **Villa Liburan**, **Kos Eksklusif / Homestay**), explore potential high-growth Jogja property zones (Sleman, Kota Jogja, Bantul), and use an interactive **"Hitung Potensi Tanah"** simulation tool to calculate estimated construction budget (RAB), room capacity, monthly cash flow, and projected ROI, then submit directly to WhatsApp for a free initial consultation.
- **Target Audience / Persona**:
  1. *Pemilik Tanah Kosong*: Owns inherited or newly purchased vacant land in Jogja that is currently generating no income.
  2. *Pemilik Kos/Homestay Lama*: Looking to renovate or add units to compete with modern student and tourist demands.
  3. *Investor Properti Luar Kota*: Capital providers seeking high yields (10–18% p.a.) in Jogja's resilient student and tourism ecosystem.
  4. *Keluarga*: Families wanting custom architectural design and stress-free turnkey contractor execution.
- **Key Value Proposition**:
  *"Dari Tanah Kosong Menjadi Properti Bernilai"* — Modern design, 100% transparent itemized RAB cost, on-time construction guarantee, and turnkey licensing (IMB/PBG) assistance.

### 2. User Experience & Visual Design

#### Key User Flows
1. **Hero & Discovery Flow**: 
   - Visitor lands on a hero section highlighting the signature question: *"Punya Tanah di Jogja? Wujudkan Menjadi Rumah, Villa atau Homestay yang Bernilai"*.
   - 4 Pillars of Trust are immediately visible: Desain Modern, Biaya Transparan, Tepat Waktu, Hasil Berkualitas.
2. **Archetype Exploration (3 Model Properti)**:
   - Interactive property preview with architectural renders, target demographics, and key economic drivers:
     - **Rumah**: Comfort for family, custom floor plans, durable finishes.
     - **Villa**: Vacation stay turned into high-occupancy daily rental investment.
     - **Homestay / Kos Eksklusif**: Long-term student & young professional passive income.
3. **Interactive Jogja Potential Zones (Peta Area Potensial)**:
   - Interactive district selector with realistic local market intelligence:
     - *Kawasan Kampus (Sleman - Depok, Condongcatur, Seturan, Babarsari, Jakal)*: High demand for Kos Eksklusif, 90%+ occupancy.
     - *Kawasan Wisata (Kaliurang, Pakem, Ngaglik, Kasihan, Sewon)*: Prime for Villas & boutique Homestays.
     - *Kawasan Premium / Hunian (Sleman, Mlati, Depok, Gamping, Banguntapan)*: Family luxury residences.
4. **Interactive Lead Form & Land Potential Calculator (Hitung Potensi Tanah)**:
   - User enters Land Area ($m^2$), Location Zone in Jogja, Target Property Type, and Initial Budget.
   - **Instant Calculation Engine** produces:
     - Maximum building footprint ($KDB / KLB$ estimates) and green open space.
     - 3 Concept Alternatives with estimated construction costs (RAB per $m^2$).
     - Kos/Villa financial projections: estimated room count, projected monthly rental income, and estimated annual ROI percentage.
   - User inputs Name & WhatsApp number; clicking **"Kirim & Konsultasi Gratis via WhatsApp"** opens WhatsApp with a neatly formatted appraisal brief ready to send to the developer team.
5. **Funnel & Process Transparency**:
   - 6-step roadmap explaining the journey from online lead, to location survey, concept & RAB discussion, permit management, and turnkey handover.
6. **Social Proof & Portfolio Showcase**:
   - Before-and-after project transformations in Jogja, client testimonials, and clear risk mitigation.

#### Visual Identity & Theme
- **Aesthetic Direction**: High-end architectural advisory. Editorial, trustworthy, and sophisticated.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Dominant Neutral (60%): Deep emerald forest (`#081C17` / `#0D2821`) for hero and dark accents; crisp ivory/cream (`#FDFBF7`) for content reading surfaces.
  - Structural Surfaces (30%): Rich hunter green (`#12362C`), warm stone slate (`#F3EFE6`), and subtle gold hairline borders (`rgba(197, 162, 93, 0.25)`).
  - Accent Budget (10%): Warm Jogja gold (`#C5A25D` / `#D4AF37`) for active buttons, key badges, and financial metrics.
- **Typography & Hierarchy**:
  - Display Font: `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Outfit` (tight letter tracking, bold architectural presence).
  - Body Font: `DM Sans` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` (15–16px, balanced readability).
  - Financial & Dimension Numerals: Tabular monospace figures (`font-mono tabular-nums`).
- **Zero-Pill Discipline & Anti-Slop**:
  - Unboxed metadata (`Depok · 92% Occupancy · Kos Eksklusif`).
  - No decorative pulsing dots, no fake system indicators, no low-opacity watermarks.
  - Top Bar follows the strict 3-zone contract with single-wordmark brand name.

### 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1: Interactive Client-Side Simulation Engine**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Calculate architectural parameters (KDB 60%, estimated floor count, room division math, per-$m^2$ standard construction grades: Standard 3.8M/m², Premium 4.8M/m², Luxury Villa 6.5M/m²) in real-time within the browser.
  - *Why*: Instant feedback encourages users to adjust sliders and fields immediately without latency or paywalls, dramatically increasing lead conversion.
- **Decision 2: Direct WhatsApp URL Integration**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Construct a pre-formatted `https://wa.me/62...` link with automated breakdown (Nama, Lokasi, Luas Tanah, Pilihan Properti, Budget, dan Hasil Estimasi).
  - *Why*: In Indonesia, especially Yogyakarta real estate, over 90% of qualified deal flow occurs directly over WhatsApp rather than cold email forms.
- **Decision 3: Integrated Lead History / CRM Preview Mode**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Include an unobtrusive toggle to preview how inbound inquiries look in the developer's funnel metrics (reflecting Section 5 & 9 in the flyer: Leads, CPL, Survey Lokasi, Proyek Closing).
  - *Why*: Demonstrates the complete end-to-end business model captured in the user's infographic.
